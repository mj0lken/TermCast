02 · Hyper Key + Trackpad

Per-Display Brightness & Volume Sliders

Remap Caps Lock to Hyper (⌃⌥⌘⇧), then hold Hyper and two-finger scroll vertically on your trackpad. Brightness targets only the display under your cursor—Apple displays via DisplayServices and external monitors via DDC/CI.

Drag windows to screen edges or corners with live Liquid Glass previews and drag-away size restore, hover any window's green zoom button for a tiling popover, or draw custom tiles on a 6×4, 12×8, or 24×12 grid.
